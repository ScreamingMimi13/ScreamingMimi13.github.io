🛒 The Block Shop

Unlock a block once and you can build with it forever. Props cost coins each time you plant one.

📋 The Job Board

Finish a job, then tap Get paid. A fresh job appears whenever you finish one.

❓ How to play

You are a little cube standing in an empty field with one block. That is the whole start.

1. Get coins. Grab the spinning coins in the grass, walk to the Quarry and hold USE to smash rocks, and finish jobs from the Job Board.

2. Spend coins. The Shop unlocks new kinds of block (buy once, build forever), sells props like trees and fountains, and has upgrades like a bigger plot of land.

3. Build. Tap Build, pick a block, then tap the ground to stack it up. Tap a block again in Erase mode to take it back.

4. Get visitors. The cooler your town looks, the higher your ✨ Wow. Wow brings 🚌 visitors, and every visitor tips you coins forever — so a good-looking town pays you while you play.

🧱 Cubeville

Start with one block. Do jobs, earn coins, buy blocks, and build a whole town out of them.
On a phone or tablet, use the stick on the left and the big round buttons on the right.
